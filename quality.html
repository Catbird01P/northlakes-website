Quality and food safety: what we do on the floor, and what we'll show you.

We're a small shop and we won't claim a certificate we don't hold. Here's what we actually run: written procedures, a batch record for every lot, tested ingredients, controlled air, and records you can audit.

Quick answer: North Lakes Copacking is an FDA-registered facility that runs dietary supplements under 21 CFR 111 and food under 21 CFR 117, following the SQF Food Safety Fundamentals code. SQF certification is in progress. Every lot has a batch record, ingredients are tested by risk, lab work is passed through at cost, and customers can review our SOPs on site.

What happens on the floor?

  • Clean air. HEPA air scrubbers run 24/7, and dust is captured at the source with HEPA vacuums.
  • Humidity control. We target under 40% relative humidity in the production zone and log it twice a day.
  • Clean lines. Machines are cleaned and checked every day: visual inspection and protein swabs before every run, and allergen tests at changeovers.
  • Every finished lot is tested. Salmonella, Listeria and E. coli on every lot, held in QC until results return. You can add tests at your expense.
  • One allergen profile at a time in production.
  • Calibrated scales, verified daily with certified, NIST-traceable test weights.
  • Lot and date code on every pack, tied to the batch record.
  • Fill weights checked and recorded every run under our net contents policy.

How are ingredients controlled?

Every incoming lot is received against a spec and held until released. Ingredients must come from vendors who have completed our approval forms, and we may reject any ingredient that has not. Higher-risk food ingredients are Salmonella-tested and held until results clear. Supplement ingredients we blend are identity-tested lot by lot under 21 CFR 111.75. See supplement co-packing and why ingredients get quarantined.

What documents can I see?

  • Our QMS document index, with every SOP and form by title and scope.
  • Our FDA registration confirmation.
  • A draft quality agreement.
  • Full SOPs, on site during an audit or visit.
  • Batch records and fill-weight records for your runs.

If something goes wrong

If a run misses spec because of our error, we own it and make it right, directly with you. A batch that fails QC isn't quietly reworked; it's investigated, documented and redone, and you hear exactly what happened.

Frequently asked questions

Are you SQF certified?

Not yet. We run to the SQF Food Safety Fundamentals code and certification is in progress. We won't claim it until we hold it.

Are you GMP certified?

There's no government GMP certificate. Third-party GMP certification isn't required by 21 CFR 111. We run to the regulations and you can audit us.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes, before any formula details change hands.

Can I visit?

Yes. Ask for a walkthrough any time.
