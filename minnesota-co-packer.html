Quick answer: North Lakes Copacking is a dry powder co-packer in Spring Park, Minnesota, in the west Minneapolis–St. Paul metro. We blend and pack powders into sachets and pouches, with stick packs from December 2026, and offer fulfillment. Runs start at 1,000 units with published pricing.

Why use a local co-packer?

  • You can see your run. Come for the first production day, check the seals and the coding, and sign off in person.
  • Cheaper, faster freight for Twin Cities and Upper Midwest brands, in both directions.
  • Easier fixes. A film or label problem gets sorted in an afternoon, not a week of email.
  • Local suppliers. Minnesota has strong ingredient, film and printing suppliers; we work with what you already use.

Who do you work with?

Food and supplement brands running 1,000–50,000 units per flavor: too big for a shared kitchen, too small for co-packers that want 35,000 units before they call back.
