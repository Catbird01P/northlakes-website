What Does Co-Packing Actually Cost in 2025?

Ole Hovde·2026-09-17

The co-packing industry has a transparency problem. Ask most facilities what it costs to run 5,000 pouches and you'll get a callback request, a discovery call, a proposal process, and — if you're lucky — a number three weeks later. We think that's backwards.

So here it is. Every number. No games.

Sachet Filling Pricing — HFFS Only

North Lakes runs horizontal form fill seal (HFFS) for powder sachets. Small runs are one flat price; larger runs are quoted on your volume, fill weight, and flavor count. Here's the full table:

VolumePricePer-Pouch Cost
1 – 8,000 sachets / stick packs$2,750 flat$0.344 or less
8,000+ sachets / stick packsCustom quoteDrops with volume

That $2,750 is a flat rate covering all sachet and stick pack fills up to 8,000 units — blending included. Whether you run 500 sachets or 8,000, your production cost is $2,750. That structure exists because our costs — machine time, setup, staffing, facility — don't change much based on whether we fill 1,000 pouches or 8,000. The floor is the floor.

Important — Sachets only

This pricing applies to roll stock sachet filling on our horizontal form fill seal machine only. Gusset pouches, zipper pouches, and bench top small runs are quoted on a project basis. Contact us with your specific format and we'll price it accurately.

Blending Costs

For runs up to 8,000 units, blending is included in the $2,750 flat rate. For larger runs or blend-only jobs, blending is $0.50–$1.50 per pound, depending on the number of ingredients, particle size, and mix time.

If you ship us a pre-blended powder ready to fill, there's nothing to blend. Your call — some brands prefer to control blending in-house, others want us to handle it. Either works.

Flavor Changeovers

Running multiple flavors in one production day is $149 per changeover. A changeover includes stopping the machine, cleaning the fill path, reloading materials, and recalibrating for the new SKU.

Important: the $149 rate applies to same-package-size changes with no allergen separation and no full clean-out. A full clean-out, allergen clean-out, or package size change is $549. We'll always tell you upfront before you commit.

What the Math Looks Like in Practice

Let's say you want to run 5,000 sachets of a pre-workout blend — pre-blended, single flavor, 30g fill:

  • Production run: $2,750
  • Blending: $0 (pre-blended)
  • Changeovers: $0 (single flavor)
  • Total: $2,750
  • Effective cost per pouch: $0.55

Now say you want to run 6,000 sachets across two flavors, same package size, and North Lakes blends both:

  • Production run, blending included: $2,750
  • Changeover: 1 x $149 = $149
  • Total: $2,899
  • Effective cost per pouch: $0.483

Above 8,000 units, pricing moves to a custom quote based on volume, fill weight, and flavor count — and per-pouch cost drops as volume goes up.

The more you run, the cheaper it gets. But the floor is low enough that small brands can afford to prove their concept.

What's Included in the Production Cost

Your $2,750 flat rate (or custom quote) covers:

  • Machine setup and calibration
  • Fill weight verification throughout the run
  • Quality checks at regular intervals
  • Bulk packout — pouches counted and packed into master cases
  • Lot numbers and batch records in your specified format

What's Not Included

  • Your bulk ingredients — you source them, they ship to us
  • Your roll stock film — you source it, it ships to us
  • Assembly (putting pouches into retail boxes) — add-on, quoted separately
  • Ecommerce fulfillment — available, quoted separately
  • Blending on runs over 8,000 units or blend-only jobs — $0.50–$1.50/lb

How This Compares to Large Co-Packers

Large facilities with 50,000-unit minimums will quote you lower per-unit tolling costs — maybe $0.18–$0.35/pouch at scale. But that comparison is misleading unless you're actually ready to order 50,000 units and can absorb the ingredient cost, storage cost, and capital tied up in that inventory.

For a brand running 5,000–15,000 sachets to prove a concept or validate a channel, our pricing is genuinely competitive with large facilities when you factor in total cost — not just tolling.

The real comparison

A 5,000-unit run at North Lakes: ~$2,750 production + your ingredient cost. The same volume at a facility with a 50,000-unit minimum: you'd need to order 10x the inventory before they'd talk to you. The per-pouch tolling might be cheaper. The total cash out is catastrophically higher.

Frequently Asked Questions About Co-Packing Cost

Is there a minimum order quantity?

For sachet HFFS filling, our minimum is any quantity — the floor price is $2,750. For gusset pouches, zipper pouches, and bench top runs, minimum and pricing are quoted on a project basis.

Do prices include blending?

Up to 8,000 units, yes — blending is included in the $2,750 flat rate. For larger runs or blend-only jobs, blending is $0.50–$1.50/lb. If you ship pre-blended product, there's nothing to blend.

What happens if my run takes more than one day?

Additional production days are priced based on your volume and format. We'll estimate your run time when you submit specs and quote accordingly.

Are there setup fees?

No separate setup fees. Machine setup and calibration are included in your production cost.

Do you charge for quality checks or documentation?

No. Lot numbers, batch records, and quality checks throughout the run are included.
