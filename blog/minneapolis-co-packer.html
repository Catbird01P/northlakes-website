Minneapolis Co-Packer — What Supplement Brands in the Midwest Need to Know

Ole Hovde·2026-09-17

North Lakes is located at 2114 Washington St NE, Minneapolis, MN 55418. We're an FDA-registered powder co-packing and fulfillment operation serving supplement and food brands nationally — and if you're based in the Midwest, a few things work particularly well in your favor.

Why Location Matters (and Where It Doesn't)

For most brands, the quality of the co-packer matters far more than their zip code. We ship finished goods to brands all over the country, and the freight difference between a facility in California, Texas, or Minnesota is typically modest relative to the production cost of a run.

That said, location creates real advantages in a few specific areas:

Freight costs for inbound materials

Your ingredients and film have to get to us somehow. If you're based in Chicago, Milwaukee, Kansas City, or anywhere in the upper Midwest, shipping to Minneapolis is often cheaper and faster than shipping to a coast. Ingredients from major Chicago-area distributors can reach us in 1–2 days via ground freight at commodity rates.

Being able to visit

There's something irreplaceable about being able to drive to your co-packer, walk the facility, and watch a run. For brands based in the Twin Cities or within a day's drive — Wisconsin, Iowa, North and South Dakota, Nebraska, northern Illinois — that's a real option. We welcome production visits.

Local supplier network

North Lakes has relationships with corrugated suppliers, freight brokers, and ingredient distributors in the Twin Cities. For brands that don't yet have these relationships built, we can make introductions. Local vendors often quote faster and communicate more reliably than national accounts for smaller volumes.

Shipping Finished Goods to Any US Market

Minneapolis is geographically central in the continental US, which means outbound freight to both coasts is relatively balanced. From our facility at 2114 Washington St NE:

  • Chicago: 1 day ground transit
  • Dallas / Houston: 2–3 days ground
  • Atlanta: 2–3 days ground
  • Los Angeles: 3–4 days ground
  • New York / Boston: 3–4 days ground

For brands doing ecommerce fulfillment through North Lakes, these transit times are relevant to your customers' experience. We ship at actual carrier cost with no markup.

FDA Registration — What It Means for Minnesota Brands

North Lakes is FDA registered. For supplement brands in Minnesota and the broader Midwest selling into retail, Amazon, or to consumers directly, FDA registration from your co-packer is increasingly required by buyers and relevant for compliance.

Minnesota has a growing supplement and functional food brand ecosystem — particularly in the Twin Cities area. If you're a local brand looking to scale from hand-packed runs to professional co-packing, North Lakes is built for exactly that transition.

What We Run

From our Minneapolis facility we run:

  • Horizontal form fill seal sachet filling — 9g to 90g fills, 3"x3" to 5.5"x9" sachets
  • Gusset pouches up to 500g fill weight
  • Weight and tumble blending per spec sheet
  • Assembly / kitting — pouches into retail boxes or bags
  • Ecommerce fulfillment — pick, pack, ship at actual carrier cost

We serve brands nationally. We're not a regional-only operation — we have clients on both coasts and everywhere in between. But if you're in the Midwest, we're in your backyard.
