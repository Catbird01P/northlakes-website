How a job runs

  1. Quote. Send product, fill weight, format, units and timeline. You get a written quote with every fee on it.
  2. Book. Sign the quote and we hold your production date. A 50% deposit and all materials are due 7 days before the run.
  3. Receive and release. We receive and inspect your ingredients and film, and test where the product type requires it.
  4. Blend, fill, code. Blended to spec, filled to our net contents policy, lot and date coded, checkweighed.
  5. Ship or fulfill. Palletized out to you, or stored here and shipped order by order.

Standard turnaround is 30 days from a signed quote to finished product.

Frequently asked questions

What does a co-packer do?

A co-packer (contract packager) makes and packages your product under your brand. You own the formula, label and customers; we blend, fill, seal, code and ship it to your spec.

What is the minimum order at North Lakes Copacking?

$2,750 flat covers up to 5,000 sachets or stick packs, counted across flavors, including machine setup and the run. Above that you pay per unit. Hand fill has no machine minimum.

What powders can you fill?

Dry, free-flowing to moderately fine powders: protein, electrolytes, greens, collagen, creatine, drink mixes, seasonings and similar. Particle size up to 1/8 inch. We don't cook, bake or add water.

Do you source ingredients and packaging?

Yes. Most brands supply their own, but we offer turnkey sourcing led by a procurement chief with 15 years in ingredients. You own everything we buy for you, including leftovers from supplier minimums.
