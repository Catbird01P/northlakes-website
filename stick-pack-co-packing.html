Why are 1 g stick packs hard to fill?

Because the legal margin is tiny. Under NIST Handbook 133, a 1 g pack can't come in under 0.9 g, and the lot average has to hit 1 g. A 0.05 g swing is 5% of the fill. We run small fills at label +2–5% and check them on a 0.001–0.01 g lab balance about every 15–30 minutes, not just a checkweigher. More in our net contents guide.

Stick packs or sachets?

Stick packs use less film per serve, pour cleanly into a bottle and look premium in a box. Sachets hold larger fills and cost less to tool for. If you're deciding, read sachet vs. stick pack, or run a sample of each.
