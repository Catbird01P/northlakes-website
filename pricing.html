Quick answer: At North Lakes Copacking, $2,750 flat covers machine setup and the full run for up to 5,000 HFFS sachets or stick packs, counted across flavors. Why is it priced this way?

  • Flat up to 5,000: setup, film threading, calibration, first-pack checks and clean-up take the same time whether we fill 500 or 5,000. That fixed work is the $2,750.
  • Per unit above 5,000: past that, you’re paying for machine time, so the price follows the count and drops as runs get bigger.
  • Sticks cost more than sachets: our stick machine runs about half as fast as the HFFS sachet line, so the same count ties up twice the machine time.
  • Counted across flavors: 5 flavors of 1,000 is a 5,000-unit run plus four $250 changeovers.

Why do you ask for 110% of materials?

Every run loses some powder and film. The losses come from:

  • machine start-up and calibration packs;
  • the small overfill that keeps every pack legal under NIST Handbook 133 (why we overfill);
  • seal and weight rejects at the checkweigher;
  • powder left in the hopper and lost at clean-out between flavors.

Those losses are mostly fixed amounts, so small runs lose a bigger share (about 10%) than large runs (about 5%). Plan on about 110% of theoretical on small runs and about 105% on large ones. Anything left over is yours.

Booking, scheduling and price lock

  • Turnaround: 30 days from a signed quote to finished product.
  • Booking fee: a flat $500 reserves your production date. It’s credited to your run invoice and non-refundable, because we held that line time for you. All ingredients, film and COAs are due at least 7 days before your run.
  • Moving your date: a flat $500, because we turned other work away to keep your slot.
  • Payment: finished product is held at our facility until your invoice is paid. Net 30 is available only by written approval, for large accounts.
  • Storage after your run: finished pallets are free for 21 days, then $45 per pallet per 30 days.
  • Price lock: pricing on your accepted quote holds for reorders of the same product for 8 months. After that, increases are capped at 5% a year, with 30 days’ notice.
  • Quotes: valid for 30 days before you accept.

Frequently asked questions

Is the $2,750 per flavor or per run?

Per run, counted across flavors. Five flavors of 1,000 is a 5,000-unit run at $2,750 plus four $250 changeovers.

Why do stick packs cost more than sachets?

Our stick machine runs about half as fast as the HFFS sachet line, so the same number of units ties up twice the machine time.

Is blending included?

No. Blending is $0.50–$1.50 per pound on top of the run price, confirmed on your quote. If you send a finished blend, there’s nothing to blend.

Who pays outbound freight?

You do, always: on your carrier account or quoted to you. Palletizing and stretch wrap on production shipments are included.

Do you mark up lab testing?

No. You pay the lab’s price plus actual shipping.

Can my price go up after I accept a quote?

Not for 8 months on reorders of the same product. After that, increases are capped at 5% a year with 30 days’ notice.
