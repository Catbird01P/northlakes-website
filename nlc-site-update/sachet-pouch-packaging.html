What is a horizontal form-fill-seal (HFFS) machine?

An HFFS machine unwinds flat printed film, folds and seals it into pouches, fills each pouch and seals the top, all in one line. Because it forms pouches from roll stock, film costs far less per unit than buying pre-made pouches. Our line is an HMC Pouchmaster. The longer version is in what is HFFS?

What film do I need?

Printed roll stock that meets our line's requirements: core size, maximum roll diameter, wind direction, web width, print repeat and eye mark. Send our roll stock film specification (PDF) to your film supplier and have them confirm each item on their quote.

Film is where most first runs go wrong. Read packaging film mistakes brands make and the $8,000 film mistake before you print.

How are pouches coded and checked?

  • Every pack is lot and date coded on a Markem-Imaje SmartDate X40 thermal transfer coder.
  • Fill weights are checkweighed or sample-weighed and recorded against our fill-weight policy.
  • Seals are checked through the run. See seal integrity.

Frequently asked questions

Can you fill pre-made pouches?

Our machine line forms pouches from roll stock. Pre-made bags can be hand-filled for small batches; tell us on the quote form.

What is the largest pouch you can fill?

Up to 1.5 kg of powder, depending on bulk density and pouch size.

How many flavors can you run in a day?

Usually 2–3, depending on clean-out between them.

What does a changeover cost?

$250 for a flavor switch at the same pouch size with no allergen. $795 for a full clean-out, which covers a pouch size change or an allergen.
