HOW IT WORKS

We run your formula. We don’t create it.

North Lakes is a co-packer. You bring a finished, tested formula and your own packaging design. We blend it, pack it, test every lot and ship it. We don’t formulate, design or approve products, and we have no in-house food scientists. That’s why our fees are lower.

Will you formulate my product?No.
Will you design my label or logo?No.
Will you test shelf life or clumping?No. You do.
Will you approve my claims?No.

Are you ready to run? Check in 60 seconds.

Not a turnkey co-packer: we don’t develop formulas or design packaging. Answer four questions. You’ll see where you stand and what to do next. Nothing is stored or sent.

The whole process, start to finish

Looking for a turnkey co-packer? We aren’t one. We’re a co-packer with procurement services. Every job follows the same path. Blue steps are yours. Green steps are ours.

  1. You

    1. Develop and finalize your formula

    With your own food scientist, formulator or supplement lab. Test it in the real package: shelf life, clumping, flow, stability, taste. We can’t take you past this point. We’re happy to make an introduction, and that’s all it is.

  2. Stop here if there’s no final formula

    No final formula? We can only quote.

    We’ll price blending, filling and packout so you can budget. We can’t sample, troubleshoot or tweak a formula, and we won’t start a run on an unfinished one.

  3. North Lakes

    2. We quote your run

    Published pricing, confirmed on a written quote. Runs start at 1,000 units and $2,750 flat covers up to 5,000. See pricing.

  4. You

    3. Sign the MSA and accept the quote

    One Master Services Agreement, signed once in our client portal. It spells out who owns what: you own the formula, the label and the results. We own the production.

  5. Both

    4. Book your production date with a $500 fee

    The flat $500 booking fee is non-refundable and credited to your run invoice. Moving a booked date is a flat $500. Paperwork and materials are due at least 7 days before the run.

  6. You or us

    5. Ingredients and packaging arrive

    Supply them yourself with lot-specific COAs, or use our procurement service: we buy exactly what you approve for a 15% fee. We order only after your payment clears: you pay us, we pay the vendor. Vendors must complete our approval forms, and we may reject any ingredient from a vendor that hasn’t.

  7. North Lakes

    6. We verify your packaging. We don’t approve it.

    Before the run we compare your packaging to your spec: the ingredient list or Supplement Facts panel matches your formula, the allergens declared match, your name and physical address are on the pack, the net quantity matches the fill target, and the lot code can be printed. That’s a match check, not a legal review. We don’t approve your label, your design or your claims. If we see a mismatch or an obvious problem, we flag it and can hold the run until you fix it. If you want us to handle packaging, we get you the die lines for a 15% handling fee.

  8. North Lakes

    7. We clean, then we run

    Every machine is cleaned and checked before every run: visual inspection and protein swabs, plus allergen tests at changeovers. Then we blend, fill, seal and code every pack. We aim over your label weight and never under, because no machine hits an exact weight on every pack. Small fills like stick packs get a bigger margin than large pouches.

  9. North Lakes

    8. Every finished lot is tested and held

    Each lot is tested for Salmonella, Listeria and E. coli and held in QC until results come back. You can add more tests at your expense. Testing is billed at the lab’s price with no markup.

  10. You

    9. Pay the invoice

    Product is held at our facility until the invoice is paid. Net 30 is by written approval only, for large accounts.

  11. North Lakes

    10. Your product ships

    On your freight account or as quoted. Finished pallets are stored free for 21 days after the run, then $45 per pallet per 30 days.

The risk is yours. If a formula that was never tested clumps in the package, that’s a formula problem, not a production problem. We make exactly what you sent, in the package you chose. That’s why every client signs the MSA before a date is booked.

Testing on every finished lot

Every finished lot gets a standard release panel: Salmonella, Listeria and generic E. coli. The lot sits in QC hold until the results are back. It’s billed at the lab’s price plus shipping, with no markup, and it’s itemized on your invoice. Want more, like heavy metals or yeast and mold? You can add them. How we control quality.
